The MIT License in this repository applies only to original code and documentation. It does not relicense datasets, publications, or third-party software.

## SNAP datasets

- **Facebook Social Circles**: J. McAuley and J. Leskovec, *Learning to Discover Social Circles in Ego Networks*, NIPS 2012. Dataset page: <https://snap.stanford.edu/data/ego-Facebook.html>.
- **Web-Google**: J. Leskovec et al., *Community Structure in Large Networks: Natural Cluster Sizes and the Absence of Large Well-Defined Clusters*, Internet Mathematics 6(1), 2009. Dataset page: <https://snap.stanford.edu/data/web-Google.html>.

The full Facebook and Web-Google datasets are not committed. Users should obtain them from SNAP and follow the provider's terms.
